📚 About This Project

Welcome to the Interactive History of Theoretical Frameworks — a comprehensive, visual exploration of intellectual history from ancient philosophy to contemporary critical theory.

🎯 What Is This?

This web application provides an interactive way to explore:

  • Theoretical frameworks that have shaped human thought
  • Key thinkers who developed influential ideas
  • Critical perspectives that challenged dominant paradigms
  • Intellectual connections across time, discipline, and geography
Coverage spans: Ancient philosophy (428 BCE) to contemporary digital humanities (2025), including 174+ entries across philosophy, historiography, sociology, critical theory, feminism, postcolonial studies, science and technology studies, and more.

✨ Features

📅 Timeline View

Explore ideas chronologically with filters for type and historical era.

🕸️ Network Graph

Visualize relationships and influences between thinkers and movements.

📊 Table View

Browse all entries with powerful search and sorting capabilities.

🏷️ Tag Explorer

Discover thematic connections through topics like feminism, marxism, or posthumanism.

🔍 Search

Find specific people, frameworks, or concepts with full-text search.

📖 Detailed Views

Click any entry for comprehensive information including key works and related figures.

🎓 Educational Purpose

This tool is designed for:

  • Students learning about intellectual history and theory
  • Researchers exploring connections across disciplines
  • Educators teaching historiography and critical theory
  • Curious minds interested in how ideas develop and influence each other

📝 Editable Database

The entire database is stored in a simple CSV file (database.csv) that you can edit:

  • Add new thinkers, frameworks, or concepts
  • Update descriptions with more detail
  • Create new connections between entries
  • Add tags to improve discoverability

See CODE_DOCUMENTATION.md for detailed instructions on editing the database.

🔬 Example Entries

Michel Foucault (Person)

French philosopher who analyzed relationships between power, knowledge, and discourse. Major works include 'Discipline and Punish' (1975) and 'The History of Sexuality' (1976-1984). Developed genealogical method and concepts of biopower and governmentality. Profoundly influenced postmodern historiography and critical theory.

Tags: post-structuralism, power-knowledge, discourse-analysis, genealogy, biopower

Donna Haraway (Person)

American biologist and feminist science studies scholar who wrote 'A Cyborg Manifesto' (1985) and 'Situated Knowledges' (1988). Challenged nature/culture binary and developed cyborg feminism and companion species theory. Emphasized staying with trouble in the Anthropocene.

Tags: cyberfeminism, feminist-sts, situated-knowledge, posthumanism, companion-species

Intersectionality (Concept)

Analytical framework coined by Kimberlé Crenshaw (1989) examining how multiple systems of oppression (race, class, gender, sexuality) intersect and create unique experiences of discrimination. Foundational to black feminist thought and contemporary critical theory.

Tags: black-feminist-thought, critical-race-theory, feminism, identity-politics
