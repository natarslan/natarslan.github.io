# Welcome

This vault powers my Quartz blog. Notes under `content/Blog` are polished essays,
`content/Drafts` holds works in progress, and the other folders surface project
logs, tool references, and attachments pulled straight from my Obsidian vault.

- Browse the latest story from `Blog/`
- Skim research tidbits and half-baked ideas in `Drafts/`
- Use `Pages/` for evergreen guides and references
- Media from `_Attachments/` is automatically embedded when linked in notes
