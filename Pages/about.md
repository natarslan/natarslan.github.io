
## hi, I'm Nat 👋

In Swedish you would pronounce my name as you would pronounce the word "night" 🌖

I analyze satellite images by day, explore the universe (both big and microscopic) by night, and somewhere in between, I make a podcast about immigrant life in Sweden. My brain is basically a 24/7 curiosity machine—I ask a lot of questions, and sometimes, I even find answers.

Oh, and I’m also a full-time parent—a job with no vacation days, infinite responsibilities. He is the best (and messiest) co-worker ever. 🚀👶

### **What I Do 🚀**
Satellite Image Analyst – I turn pixels into insights, decoding the Earth from above.
Podcaster-in-the-Making 🎙️ – Sharing stories of immigrants in Sweden, the good, the weird, and the “huh, that’s different.”
Lover of the Small & the Vast – Black-and-white photography, microscope observations, deep dives into sci-fi worlds.

### **Why? Because Curiosity Killed the Cat... but Satisfaction Brought It Back 🧐**
I’ve always been the kind of person who needs to know why and how things work—whether it’s a satellite image, a tiny organism under a microscope, or Swedish social norms that make no sense to newcomers.
