🔍 **Exploring the Weather of 1984 During the Miners' Strike** 

The summer of 1984 is remembered by many for the **Miners' Strike**—a pivotal moment in UK history. But what role did the weather play in shaping people's experiences? In my latest project, I dive into historical weather data to uncover how this summer compared to "normal" conditions. Using **temperature**, **rainfall**, and **sunshine** data, I explore the weather of 1984 during the strikes.

Check out the interactive visualizations and find out what the data reveals about this crucial summer!
