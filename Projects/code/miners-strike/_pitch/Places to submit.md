

## 🗞 **UK Mainstream Media & Magazines**

### **1. The Guardian – Data, Features, or Opinion**

- Audience: General public, progressives, climate-aware readers.
- Fit: Historical + climate + labor memory = very on brand.
- Submission: [Guardian Pitch Guide](https://www.theguardian.com/info/2015/aug/26/how-to-pitch-to-the-opinion-desk) or contact section editors directly via email/Twitter.

### **2. The Conversation UK**

- Audience: Educated general public; publishes articles by experts/academics.
- Fit: If you can frame your article as expert commentary (data journalism counts!).
- Submission: [https://theconversation.com/uk](https://theconversation.com/uk)

### **3. Prospect Magazine (Sent) **

- Audience: Policy, politics, culture readers.
- Fit: Great if you emphasize implications—how climate memory intersects with labor history.
- Submission: Email pitch to webdesk@prospect-magazine.co.uk

### **4. New Statesman**

- Audience: Left-leaning, political readers.
- Fit: If you include a more narrative/political angle.
- Submission: Email pitch to: [submissions@newstatesman.co.uk](mailto:submissions@newstatesman.co.uk)

### **5. iNews / The i Paper(sent) **

- Audience: General UK news readers, often features niche data stories.
- Fit: They publish explainer-style data stories and climate retrospectives.
- Submission: Look for relevant editors on Twitter or contact via [inews.co.uk](https://inews.co.uk)

## 📈 **Data Journalism Platforms**

### **6. DataJournalism.com** (by European Journalism Centre)

- Audience: Journalists and media professionals.
- Fit: Ideal to showcase your method and findings; not for mainstream audience, more peer visibility.
- Submission: Blog or case study format via [contact form](https://datajournalism.com/about/contact)

### **7. Tableau Public Gallery / Observable / Flourish Stories**

- Audience: Data viz fans and journalists.
- Fit: If you want to make your article visually compelling and shareable, publish on these platforms with narrative captions.

## 🌍 **Climate & Environment Media**

### **8. Carbon Brief (Sent) **

- Audience: Policy-makers, scientists, climate-aware readers.
- Fit: They’ve done retrospectives on historic weather events. Worth pitching especially if your data analysis is rigorous.
- Submission: Email editors via [https://www.carbonbrief.org/about/](https://www.carbonbrief.org/about/)

### **9. Grist** (US-based, but open to global climate + justice stories)(Sent)

- Audience: Climate activists, policy-interested readers.
- Fit: If you emphasize heat + labor justice angle.
- Submission: pitches@grist.org
### **10. Inside Climate News(sent) **

- Audience: Global, investigative climate stories.
- Fit: Maybe a long shot unless you tie the story to modern implications, but they love climate + human impact stories.
- Submission: https://insideclimatenews.org/contact/

## 🧑‍🏫 **History & Labour Outlets**

### **11. History Workshop Online(sent)**

- Audience: Public historians, leftist historians, labour history buffs.
- Fit: Perfect for labor memory + data angle. They love unusual source material.
- Submission: [Pitch guidelines here](https://www.historyworkshop.org.uk/pitching-guidelines/)

### **12. Red Pepper Magazine(sent) **

- Audience: UK leftists, unions, activists.
- Fit: High alignment if your article critiques or reflects on the politics of climate, class, and memory.
- Submission: editors@redpepper.org.uk
### **13. Tribune(sent) **

- Audience: Socialist/left readers in the UK.
- Fit: Loves stories about working-class history with a modern hook.
- Submission: Email: [info@tribunemag.co.uk](mailto:info@tribunemag.co.uk)

## 🧾 **Special Mentions & Local Angles**

### **14. Nation.Cymru**

- Audience: Welsh national and regional readers.
- Fit: If one of your locations is in Wales, _especially South Wales_, this is a great local hook.
- Submission: [editor@nation.cymru](mailto:editor@nation.cymru)

### **15. Big Issue**

- Audience: General public, with a strong interest in social justice, homelessness, and inequality.
- Fit: Human-interest storytelling with data = good combo for them.
- Submission: [editorial@bigissue.com](mailto:editorial@bigissue.com)
