## Baby Car Seat Certifications and Safety Features


| **Name**                                  | **What It Measures (Bullet Points)**                                                                                                                                               | **What It Means for the User**                                                                      | **Country/Organization**             | **Original Test Webpage**                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **UN R129 (i-Size)**                      | - Side-impact protection (mandatory) <br> - Rearward-facing until at least 15 months <br> - ISOFIX only (no seatbelt installation) <br> - Height-based classification (not weight) | Safer side-impact protection, longer rear-facing, easier correct installation, reduced misuse risk. | UNECE (United Nations)               | [UN R129 Regulation](https://unece.org/DAM/trans/doc/2014/wp29grsp/GRSP-56-24r1e.pdf)                                              |
| **UN R44 (ECE R44)**                      | - Weight-based classification (Group 0/0+/1/2/3) <br> - Frontal collision tested <br> - Optional side-impact tests                                                                 | Older standard, allows seatbelt installation, less strict on side impacts. Still widely accepted.   | UNECE (United Nations)               | [UN R44 Regulation](https://unece.org/sites/default/files/2021-07/R044r3am11e.pdf)                                                 |
| **SICT (Side Impact Cushion Technology)** | - Extra energy-absorbing cushions on sides <br> - Redforces side-impact protection beyond i-Size                                                                                   | Enhanced side-impact safety, often found on high-end seats (e.g., Britax, Cybex).                   | Independent (Brand-Specific)         | [Wiki](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Side_Impact_Protection_System)                                                         |
| **Plus Test (Sweden)**                    | - Strict frontal & rear-facing crash test <br> - Measures neck load in collisions <br> - Focus on rear-facing seats                                                                | Ensures minimal neck strain, popular for extended rear-facing seats (e.g., Axkid, Besafe).          | Sweden (Folksam Insurance)           | [Plus Test Info](https://www.vti.se/en/services/laboratory-and-testing/crash-safety-testing/child-restraint-systems/the-plus-test) |
| **ADAC Test**                             | - Independent crash tests (frontal/side/rear) <br> - Evaluates safety, ergonomics, handling, pollutants                                                                            | Highly trusted rating; seats with high ADAC scores are among the safest.                            | Germany (ADAC)                       | [Wiki](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/ADAC#Testing_and_technical_services)                                                   |
| **5-Point Harness**                       | - Secures child at shoulders, hips, and between legs <br> - Distributes crash forces evenly                                                                                        | Prevents ejection, safer than seatbelt-only seats for young kids.                                   | Universal (Industry Standard)        | N/A                                                                                                                                |
| **Load Leg**                              | - Metal leg extending from seat to car floor <br> - Reduces forward rotation in crashes                                                                                            | Adds stability, reduces head movement in frontal crashes.                                           | Varies by brand (e.g., Nuna, BeSafe) | N/A                                                                                                                                |

### Extra Notes:
- **i-Size (R129)** is the newest and strictest UN standard, replacing **R44** gradually.  
- **SICT/Load Leg** are brand-specific upgrades, not certifications.  
- **ADAC/Plus Test** are voluntary but highly respected.
